# Code of Conduct

Spotriq contributors are expected to keep technical discussion professional, specific and constructive.

We welcome good-faith disagreement about architecture, evidence, safety, product decisions and implementation. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of another person's private information, or knowingly publishing secrets/exploits that put users at risk are not acceptable.

When raising a concern, focus on observable behavior, reproducible evidence and concrete impact. Security-sensitive issues should be disclosed privately according to `SECURITY.md`.

Project maintainers may moderate or remove contributions that violate these expectations in order to protect contributors, users and the integrity of the project.
